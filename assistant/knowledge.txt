# Garage Meier: facts for the phone assistant

Garage Meier is a fictional independent car repair workshop used for the Suisse Line application challenge.

## Contact and location
- Address: Garage Meier, Zurich-Altstetten (fictional)
- Workshop phone for urgent transfers: set in Call Forwarding
- Parking: 6 customer spaces in front of the workshop
- Public transport: 5 minutes from Zurich Altstetten station

## Opening hours (Europe/Zurich)
- Monday to Friday: 07:30 to 12:00 and 13:15 to 18:00
- Saturday: 08:00 to 12:00 (tyre changes and small services only)
- Sunday and public holidays: closed
- Car drop-off: from 07:15. Key box at the door for drop-off outside opening hours.

## Services, appointment length and prices (incl. 8.1% VAT)
| Service | Appointment length | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis (warning light, noise, unclear problem) | 45 min | CHF 120, credited if we do the repair |
| Small service (oil, filter, check) | 60 min | from CHF 290 |
| Large service (manufacturer schedule) | 3 h, car stays the day | from CHF 590 |
| Tyre change, wheels on rims | 30 min | CHF 80 |
| Tyre change, tyres on rims (fitting and balancing) | 60 min | CHF 140 |
| Tyre storage per season | none | CHF 60 |
| Brake pads, one axle | 90 min | from CHF 320 |
| Air conditioning service | 60 min | CHF 149 |
| Vehicle inspection preparation (MFK) | half a day | from CHF 180, plus official test fee |
| Battery test and replacement | 30 min | test free, battery from CHF 190 |

Large repairs (engine, gearbox, clutch, timing belt, accident damage) always start with a diagnosis and a written quote. The customer approves the quote before work starts.

## Brands
All common brands. Specialised in VW group (VW, Audi, Skoda, Seat, Cupra), Toyota and hybrid cars. No work on high-voltage battery packs of electric cars; other EV service is fine.

## Courtesy car and pickup
- Courtesy car: CHF 30 per day, book at least one day ahead, limited availability.
- Free e-bike to Altstetten station.
- Pickup and delivery within Zurich: CHF 40.

## Payment
Card, TWINT or invoice (30 days) for regular customers. Payment when the car is picked up.

## Warranty
12 months on parts and labour. Manufacturer warranty stays valid with our service.

## Status words (repair_status tool)
- received: the car has arrived
- diagnosing: a mechanic is checking the car
- waiting approval: a quote is ready; the customer must say yes before work continues
- waiting parts: parts are ordered
- in repair: work in progress
- ready: ready for pickup during opening hours
- picked up: already collected
